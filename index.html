High-Protein & Popular

High-Protein Chicken & Quinoa Bowl

Prep & Cook: 25 mins • Balanced Meal

Ingredients

  • 1 chicken breast, seasoned with olive oil, garlic, salt & pepper
  • 1/2 cup cooked quinoa (or brown rice)
  • 1 cup broccoli florets & sliced bell peppers
  • 1 tbsp olive oil + squeeze of fresh lemon

Steps

  1. Pan-sear or grill the chicken until cooked through, then slice.
  2. Lightly steam or sauté the broccoli and bell peppers.
  3. Assemble over quinoa and drizzle with olive oil and lemon.
Classic & Natural

Traditional Lentil & Vegetable Stew

Prep & Cook: 35 mins • Cozy & Fiber-Rich

Ingredients

  • 1 cup green or brown lentils, rinsed
  • 1 diced onion, 1 grated tomato, 1 chopped carrot
  • Fresh parsley, cilantro, cumin, ginger & salt
  • 1.5 tbsp olive oil + water or light broth

Steps

  1. Sauté onion, carrot, and spices in olive oil for 3–4 minutes.
  2. Add lentils, tomato, herbs, and water; simmer until tender.
  3. Serve warm with a touch of fresh herbs.
Quick & Fresh

Mediterranean Chickpea & Herb Salad

Prep Time: 10 mins • Light & Filling

Ingredients

  • 1.5 cups cooked chickpeas
  • 1 diced cucumber & 1 cup cherry tomatoes
  • Fresh mint or parsley, olive oil, lemon juice & salt

Steps

  1. Combine chickpeas, cucumber, tomatoes, and chopped herbs in a bowl.
  2. Toss with olive oil, lemon juice, and salt just before serving.
Traditional Grain

Old-School Barley & Herb Dish

Prep & Cook: 30 mins • Wholesome Classic

Ingredients

  • 1 cup whole barley or cracked barley, rinsed
  • 2.5 cups water, 1 tbsp olive oil, pinch of salt & cumin
  • Dried thyme or fresh herbs to finish

Steps

  1. Simmer barley in water with salt and cumin until liquid is absorbed.
  2. Stir in olive oil and herbs, cover for 5 minutes, and serve.
Omega-3 & Wholesome

Baked Salmon with Sweet Potato & Greens

Prep & Bake: 25 mins • Sheet-Pan Meal

Ingredients

  • 1–2 salmon fillets + 1 cubed sweet potato
  • Handful of spinach or green beans
  • Olive oil, garlic powder, lemon slices, salt & pepper

Steps

  1. Toss sweet potato cubes with oil and seasoning; bake at 200°C (400°F) for 12 mins.
  2. Add salmon and greens to the pan and bake 10–12 mins more until done.
